## 11. Development Rules

- Follow the existing project conventions.
- Reuse components where appropriate.
- Keep components modular and maintainable.
- Separate content data from presentation where useful.
- Use TypeScript types consistently if the project uses TypeScript.
- Avoid unnecessary dependencies.
- Avoid overengineering.
- Do not introduce complex state management for static content.
- Preserve working integrations.
- Ensure forms, links and navigation actually work.
- Avoid placeholder content in production.
- Do not leave broken links or unfinished sections.

Do not modify unrelated backend functionality or integrations merely to achieve the visual redesign.
